Compare 17 campground records around Lake Crescent, Port Angeles, Sequim, Forks, the Sol Duc corridor, and the Strait of Juan de Fuca.

Clallam County spans dry shoreline near Sequim, Lake Crescent, temperate forest around Forks, and the northwest coast. This guide brings the choices into one consistent view. How to choose

Choose Log Cabin or Sol Duc when hookups matter. Fairholme is the classic Lake Crescent dry-camping base, while Mora puts you closest to Rialto Beach. Heart O’ the Hills works well for Port Angeles and Hurricane Ridge. Salt Creek and Dungeness add reservable county sites with showers; Sequim Bay adds full hookups. Bear Creek, Lyre River, and Sadie Creek are small first-come DNR choices for self-contained rigs, with seasonal status and road conditions worth checking before departure.
