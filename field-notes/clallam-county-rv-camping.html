How to choose

Choose Sequim or Port Angeles private parks for services near town. Log Cabin, Sol Duc, and Sequim Bay cover the strongest public hookup options. Fairholme is the classic Lake Crescent dry-camping base, while Mora works for Rialto Beach. For the outer coast, 3 Rivers bridges Forks and La Push, Quileute Oceanside reaches First Beach, and the Sekiu and Neah Bay parks trade a longer drive for direct Strait or Pacific access. Bear Creek, Lyre River, and Sadie Creek remain small first-come choices for self-contained rigs.

Accuracy note: A campground-wide maximum can describe only a few pads. When an operator does not publish one, this guide says to confirm fit rather than filling the gap with an estimate. Drive times use an all-road Seattle baseline and round upward for towing. Temporary closures, fire restrictions, tribal permits, and first-come availability can change after this review date.
