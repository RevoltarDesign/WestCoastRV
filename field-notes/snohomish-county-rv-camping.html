How to choose

Choose Lake Pleasant or Maple Grove for the shortest urban approach. Angel of the Winds offers the largest verified private inventory and the broadest modern resort facilities. Lake Ki and Lake Goodwin Resort put swimming, fishing, and paddling at the campsite, while Wenberg provides the most varied public lake loops. Kayak Point is the saltwater choice. Thunderbird and Cascade Views serve the US 2 corridor; Flowing Lake works for a quieter foothill lake. River Meadows and Squire Creek trade hookups for simpler settings, while Whitehorse supplies full utilities near Darrington.

Accuracy note: Utility types and rig fit can vary by site. A blank value means the current operator did not publish the fact; it does not mean the amenity is absent. Drive estimates start with an all-road route from central Seattle and round upward for towing and common Puget Sound or US 2 delays. Check live operator details before choosing a pad.
